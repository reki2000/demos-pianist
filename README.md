# pianist

Live demo: https://reki2000.github.io/demos-pianist/

![Cinematic view](docs/screenshots/cinematic.png)

A WebGL recital: a stylized 3D pianist plays 18 pieces with automatically generated fingering, synced to a recorded Steinway grand. No motion capture — every keystroke is planned by the motion engine in `dist/motion.js`.

A standalone single-file build, `piano-recital.html` (generated by `python pack-v8.py`, not committed), embeds the audio and needs no internet connection.

## Controls

- Pick one of 18 pieces at the bottom; play/pause, restart, seek, speed, volume.
- Views: Cinematic (5 shots), Hands, Wide, Pedal (damper close-up), Free (drag/scroll).
- Space toggles play/pause; bottom-right button for fullscreen.

## Instruments

The switcher at the top selects Piano / Guitar / Violin / Cello. All four share the song list, transport, cameras and score panel; all performers use the shared transport and cameras. The string instruments live in `dist/instruments/` (copies of the demos-guitarist / demos-violinist / demos-cellist modules with host audio, geometry and repertoire adaptations recorded in `audio-lifecycle-patches.json` / `geometry-patches.json` / `repertoire-patches.json`) and are driven by `dist/instruments/shell.js`; violin and cello scores are drawn by `dist/instruments/staff-score.js` from each module's `scoreConfig`.

| Instrument | Pieces | Sound | Fingering |
|---|---|---|---|
| Piano | 18 | Recorded Steinway samples | `dist/motion.js` |
| Classical Guitar | 6 | Karplus-Strong nylon | Beam search, precomputed (below) |
| Violin | 4 | Bowed-string synth | Position/string engine + bow plan |
| Cello | 4 | Bowed-string synth | Position/string engine + bow plan |

Guitar fingering takes seconds per piece, so plans ship precomputed in `dist/instruments/guitar-plans.js` and are injected before planning; regenerate with `node precompute-guitar-plans.cjs` (it also proves the cached plans reproduce the live plans' poses exactly). Note the renderer mirrors X (`gl.js` `toWorld`): the string modules are authored for that frame, and `node integration-test.cjs` pins the *drawn* figures' handedness (bow/pluck wrist on the anatomical right) against the integrated copies, plus byte-identity with the module repos.

Violin left-hand trajectories ship in `dist/instruments/violin-hand-frames.js`: one wrist-rooted skeleton, four left fingers shortened by 20% with fixed bone lengths, articulated thumb, and analytic fingertip contacts. Regenerate offline with `node precompute-violin-hand.cjs`; playback never runs the nonlinear fitter. `node violin-left-hand-test.cjs` checks all four pieces and the rendered hand geometry. See [references and verification](docs/verification/violin-left-hand/README.md) and the [anatomical left-hand orientation correction](docs/verification/violin-left-orientation/README.md). All five digits, palm and wrist share one connected skin surface in one reusable dynamic buffer; [hand surface verification](docs/verification/hand-joins/README.md) covers shared seams and buffer lifetime.

The violin repertoire includes the complete Bach Partita No. 3 **Preludio (BWV 1006)** at quarter = 120, with alternating short bow strokes. **Recuerdos de la Alhambra** uses TAB-checked second-string opening melody, left-hand hints from the engraved score, and thumb accompaniment with a–m–i tremolo. Existing guitar note timing and pitches are preserved. Rebuild these adaptations with `node build-repertoire.cjs`, then regenerate their offline plans/hand caches. See [sources and licences](repertoire-sources/README.md) and [repertoire verification](docs/verification/repertoire/README.md).

Violin and cello right hands use five articulated digits anchored to the frog, with instrument-specific little-finger placement. All five digits, palm and wrist use one connected static mesh per grip; [skin verification](docs/verification/bow-skin/README.md) includes before/after images and buffer checks. References and original grip validation: [bow-hold verification](docs/verification/bow-hold/README.md).

Piano hands share the B proportions for the four fingers and palm, with a shorter articulated thumb and connected skin. The piano stage is normalized to a 23.5 mm white-key pitch. Unreachable chords are rolled with synchronized sound/keys and short pedal bridges, following small-hand performance techniques. All source pitches and fingerings remain; [piano hand verification](docs/verification/piano-hands/README.md) documents dimensions, timing adaptations and resource checks. Regenerate the trajectories offline with `node precompute-piano-hands.cjs`.

Guitar hands now use the same B skeleton and connected palm/wrist skin on both sides, with offline trajectories and fixed bone lengths. The classical guitar is normalized to a 650 mm scale and a 490 × 370 × 99 mm body; its neck, fingerboard and soundhole are aligned to those dimensions. See [guitar hand references, screenshots and verification](docs/verification/guitar-hands/README.md) and [finger crossing / barre handoff checks](docs/verification/guitar-finger-clearance/README.md).

## Motion

Fingering minimizes unnatural spans (Parncutt et al. 1997 comfortable/practical maxima), lateral splay, and hand travel; free fingers keep a narrower lateral range and stay ordered. Fingers prepare up to 180 ms before a strike and relax over ~160 ms after release; distal joints (PIP/DIP) trail the knuckle (MCP) with a slight phase lag so fingers curl instead of swinging rigidly. Free fingers are eased between frames (`PianoMotion.soften`); bone lengths never change. Wrist lift/flexion, lean, and head nods follow dynamics and phrase intensity. Audio, keys, and fingertips share the Web Audio clock.

MIDI voices are redistributed to two playable hands by span and travel distance; a few unplayable chords are rolled. Pitch, velocity, and note counts are preserved, so timing is not a bit-exact reproduction of the source MIDI.

## Verification

`node verify.cjs` checks all 18 pieces: fixed skeleton at 777 samples, fingertip contact error ≤ ~5 mm, 2,016 continuous in-between frames, pedal events, audio/clock sync, and UI. More: `anatomy/collision/continuity/score/pedal/expressive/direction/fingering/transition/hanging/audio-test.cjs`. `node integration-test.cjs` covers the multi-instrument shell and the string modules' drawn-frame checks. Fingering research: `FINGERING-NOTES.md`; history: `docs/DEVELOPMENT-NOTES.md`.

## Build & dev

- Serve the multi-file build: `python -m http.server 8000 --directory dist`
- After changing `dist/motion.js`: `node add-songs.cjs --replan` (re-plans all 18 pieces), then `node verify.cjs`
- Re-convert source MIDI: `python convert.py` (overwrites fingering — replan afterwards)
- GitHub Actions runs `verify.cjs` + `audio-test.cjs`, builds `piano-recital.html`/`.zip`, and deploys to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
